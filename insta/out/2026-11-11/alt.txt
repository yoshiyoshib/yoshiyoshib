Slide 2: apprehensive — （これから起こることが）不安な、心配な. Worried or nervous about something that is going to happen. Example: I was apprehensive about speaking in front of so many people.
Slide 3: elated — 大喜びの、有頂天の. Extremely happy and excited, often because of good news. Example: She was elated when she got the job offer.
Slide 4: indignant — （不当な扱いに）憤慨した. Angry because something seems unfair. Example: He was indignant at being blamed for the mistake.
Slide 5: wistful — （もう手に入らないものを思って）切なげな. Slightly sad because you are thinking about something you no longer have. Example: She gave a wistful smile as she looked at the old photos.
Slide 6: ambivalent — 複雑な気持ちの、気持ちが揺れている. Having mixed feelings about something. Example: I feel ambivalent about moving abroad.
