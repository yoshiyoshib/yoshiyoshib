Slide 2: beat around the bush — 遠回しに言う、本題を避ける. To avoid saying something directly. Example: Stop beating around the bush and tell me what happened.
Slide 3: hit the nail on the head — まさに的を射る. To describe exactly what is causing a problem or situation. Example: You hit the nail on the head with that comment.
Slide 4: have a bone to pick — （人に）文句・言いたいことがある. To have a complaint that you want to discuss with someone. Example: I have a bone to pick with you about the bill.
Slide 5: spill the beans — （秘密を）もらす、ばらす. To tell people something that was supposed to be secret. Example: Who spilled the beans about the surprise party?
Slide 6: get a word in edgewise — （相手が話し続ける中で）なんとか口をはさむ. To manage to say something when someone else is talking a lot. Example: He talked so much I couldn't get a word in edgewise.
