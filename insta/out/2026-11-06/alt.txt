Slide 2: cost an arm and a leg — とんでもなく高くつく. To be extremely expensive. Example: Fixing the car is going to cost an arm and a leg.
Slide 3: tighten your belt — 出費を切り詰める. To spend less money because you have less than before. Example: With prices rising, we all need to tighten our belts.
Slide 4: break the bank — 大金がかかる（否定文で「そこまで高くない」）. To cost more than you can afford. Usually used in negative sentences. Example: A weekend trip doesn't have to break the bank.
Slide 5: foot the bill — 費用を負担する. To pay for something, often something expensive. Example: The company will foot the bill for your training.
Slide 6: make ends meet — なんとか生活をやりくりする. To have just enough money to pay for the things you need. Example: She works two jobs to make ends meet.
