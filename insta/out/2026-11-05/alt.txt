Slide 2: pencil in — （仮に）予定を入れる. To arrange something for a time, knowing the plan might change later. Example: Let's pencil in a meeting for Thursday afternoon.
Slide 3: iron out — （問題・細かい点を）解決する、詰める. To solve the small problems or disagreements that remain in a plan. Example: We still need to iron out a few details before the launch.
Slide 4: fall through — （計画・取引が）だめになる、立ち消えになる. If a plan or deal falls through, it fails to happen. Example: Our trip fell through at the last minute.
Slide 5: move up — （予定を）前倒しにする. To change an event to an earlier date or time. Example: Can we move the deadline up to Friday?
Slide 6: mull over — じっくり考える、熟考する. To think carefully about something for a period of time before deciding. Example: Let me mull it over and get back to you tomorrow.
