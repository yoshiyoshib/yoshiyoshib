Slide 2: meticulous — 細部まで注意を払う、几帳面な. Very careful and paying close attention to every detail. Example: She keeps meticulous records of every meeting.
Slide 3: candid — 率直な、遠慮のない. Honest and direct, even when the truth may be uncomfortable. Example: Thanks for being so candid about the problems.
Slide 4: resilient — 立ち直りが早い、打たれ強い. Able to recover quickly after something difficult or unpleasant. Example: The team proved remarkably resilient after the setback.
Slide 5: pragmatic — 現実的な、実際的な. Dealing with problems in a sensible way based on what is possible. Example: We need a pragmatic solution, not a perfect one.
Slide 6: complacent — 現状に満足しきった、油断した. Too satisfied with your situation, so you stop trying to improve. Example: After two good years, the company became complacent.
