Slide 2: perfidious — 裏切りの、背信的な. Guilty of deliberately betraying someone who trusted you. Example: The country's perfidious ambassador was secretly passing data to a rival state.
Slide 3: nefarious — 極悪な、非道な. Wicked and criminal; deliberately immoral or harmful. Example: The gang's nefarious activities were finally exposed by an undercover reporter.
Slide 4: vindictive — 復讐心の強い、意地悪な. Having a strong desire to hurt or punish someone who has wronged you. Example: She left a vindictive review after her former employer let her go.
Slide 5: recalcitrant — 頑固に反抗する、言うことを聞かない. Stubbornly refusing to follow rules or accept authority. Example: The recalcitrant witness refused to answer the prosecutor's questions.
Slide 6: duplicitous — 二枚舌の、表裏のある. Behaving in a deliberately deceptive way; saying one thing while doing another. Example: His duplicitous campaign relied on telling each voter group what it wanted to hear.
