Slide 2: step down — （役職から）退く、辞任する. To leave an important job or position. Example: The CEO will step down at the end of the year.
Slide 3: take on — （仕事・責任を）引き受ける. To accept a job or responsibility. Example: I can't take on any more projects this month.
Slide 4: lay off — （業績などの理由で）解雇する. To end someone's job because there is not enough work or money. Example: The factory laid off 200 workers last month.
Slide 5: burn out — 燃え尽きる、疲れ果てる. To become exhausted from working too hard for too long. Example: If you never take a break, you'll burn out.
Slide 6: hand over — （仕事・責任を）引き継ぐ、引き渡す. To give responsibility for something to someone else. Example: Before I go on leave, I'll hand over my clients to Ken.
