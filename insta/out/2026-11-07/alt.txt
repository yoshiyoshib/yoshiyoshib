Slide 2: ubiquitous — どこにでもある、至るところにある. Seeming to be everywhere at the same time. Example: Smartphones have become ubiquitous in daily life.
Slide 3: exacerbate — （悪い状況を）さらに悪化させる. To make a bad situation even worse. Example: The heat wave exacerbated the water shortage.
Slide 4: ostensibly — 表向きは、建前としては. As it appears or is claimed, though this may not be the real reason. Example: He left early, ostensibly to catch a train.
Slide 5: intransigent — 頑として譲らない. Refusing to change your opinion or behavior. Example: Both sides remained intransigent, so the talks collapsed.
Slide 6: ephemeral — はかない、つかの間の. Lasting for only a very short time. Example: Online trends are often ephemeral.
