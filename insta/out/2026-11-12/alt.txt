Slide 2: come down with — （病気に）かかる、なりかける. To start to have an illness, usually not a serious one. Example: I think I'm coming down with a cold.
Slide 3: shake off — （病気・不調を）振り払う、治す. To get rid of an illness or a bad feeling. Example: I can't seem to shake off this cough.
Slide 4: wear off — （効果が）切れる、薄れる. To slowly become weaker and then stop. Example: The painkillers started to wear off after a few hours.
Slide 5: pass out — 気を失う. To suddenly become unconscious for a short time. Example: He almost passed out from the heat.
Slide 6: bounce back — （元気に）立ち直る、すぐ回復する. To recover quickly after an illness or a difficult time. Example: Kids usually bounce back quickly after a fever.
