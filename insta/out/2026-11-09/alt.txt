Slide 2: warm up to — （人に）だんだん打ち解ける. To gradually start to like someone or feel comfortable with them. Example: The kids took a while to warm up to their new teacher.
Slide 3: drift apart — （だんだん）疎遠になる. To slowly become less close to someone over time. Example: We drifted apart after she moved to Osaka.
Slide 4: patch up — （関係を）修復する、仲直りする. To end an argument and become friendly again. Example: They finally patched things up after a long talk.
Slide 5: talk down to — 見下した話し方をする. To speak to someone as if they were less intelligent than you. Example: I hate it when managers talk down to new staff.
Slide 6: stand up for — （人を）かばう、擁護する. To defend or support someone when others criticize them. Example: Thanks for standing up for me in the meeting.
