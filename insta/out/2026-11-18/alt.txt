Slide 2: compelling — 説得力のある、思わず引きつけられる. So convincing or interesting that people pay attention. Example: She made a compelling case for a four-day week.
Slide 3: plausible — もっともらしい、ありえそうな. Seeming reasonable or likely to be true. Example: His excuse sounded plausible, but I wasn't sure.
Slide 4: contentious — 議論を呼ぶ、意見が分かれる. Likely to cause disagreement or argument. Example: Immigration remains a contentious issue.
Slide 5: tentative — 仮の、暫定的な. Not certain or final yet. Example: We reached a tentative agreement on the budget.
Slide 6: coherent — 筋の通った、一貫した. Clear, logical and easy to understand. Example: We need a coherent plan, not a list of ideas.
