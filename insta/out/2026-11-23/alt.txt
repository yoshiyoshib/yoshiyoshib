Slide 2: set off — 出発する、旅立つ. To begin a journey or trip. Example: We set off for Paris at dawn to catch the first train.
Slide 3: see off — （人を）見送る. To go somewhere to say goodbye to someone who is leaving. Example: Her whole family came to see her off at the airport.
Slide 4: touch down — （飛行機が）着陸する. When a plane touches down, it lands on the ground. Example: The flight touched down in Seoul thirty minutes early.
Slide 5: head back — 帰路につく、戻る. To return to a place you came from. Example: After a week in Kyoto, we headed back to Tokyo.
Slide 6: stop over — （旅の途中で）立ち寄る. To make a short stay somewhere on the way to your final destination. Example: We stopped over in Dubai for two nights on the way to London.
