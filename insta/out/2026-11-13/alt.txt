Slide 3: call it a day — （仕事などを）今日はここまでにする. To stop working on something, either for the rest of the day or for good. Example: It's almost six. Let's call it a day.
Slide 4: once in a blue moon — ごくまれに. Very rarely. Example: I only eat fast food once in a blue moon.
Slide 5: against the clock — 時間に追われて、急いで. Trying to finish something before a deadline. Example: The team is working against the clock to fix the bug.
Slide 6: at the eleventh hour — 土壇場で、最後の最後に. At the last possible moment. Example: The deal was signed at the eleventh hour.
