Slide 2: brush up on — （忘れかけた知識・技術を）磨き直す. To practice or study something again to improve it. Example: I need to brush up on my Spanish before the trip.
Slide 3: catch on — （やっと）理解する、わかってくる. To begin to understand something. Example: It took me a while to catch on to the rules.
Slide 4: cram for — （試験のために）詰め込み勉強をする. To study a lot in a short time just before an exam. Example: He stayed up all night cramming for the test.
Slide 5: fall behind — 遅れをとる、ついていけなくなる. To fail to keep up with others or with a schedule. Example: If you miss class, it's easy to fall behind.
Slide 6: read up on — （前もって）よく調べる、読んで勉強する. To read a lot about a subject to learn about it. Example: I read up on the company before my interview.
